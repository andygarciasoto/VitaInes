## APP DESCRIPTION
*(Max 4,000 characters — copy and paste exactly as written)*

---

**VitaInes — Blood Pressure & Heart Health Tracker**

Know your numbers. Own your health.

VitaInes is your personal blood pressure companion — designed to help you track, understand, and take control of your heart health every single day. Whether you're managing hypertension, working with your doctor, or simply staying proactive about your wellness, VitaInes makes it simple, beautiful, and meaningful.

---

**📊 BLOOD PRESSURE TRACKING**
Log your systolic, diastolic, and pulse in seconds. Every reading is instantly classified — Normal, Elevated, High, or Call Doctor — so you always know exactly where you stand. Add notes, set the date and time, and build a complete picture of your cardiovascular health.

**📈 TRENDS & INTERACTIVE CHARTS**
Beautiful charts show your blood pressure trends over 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, or a full year. Filter by reading status, compare systolic and diastolic lines, and spot patterns before they become problems. Visual health data that actually makes sense.

**💊 MEDICATION MANAGEMENT**
Add your blood pressure medications, set personalized daily reminders, and never miss a dose. Track active and paused medications in one organized place, with notes from your doctor. Stay consistent — because consistency is what moves the needle.

**🤖 AI HEALTH INSIGHTS**
Receive personalized health recommendations powered by AI — from nutrition and hydration tips to exercise guidance and stress management — all tailored to your actual readings and trends. Smart wellness, not generic advice.

**🔔 SMART REMINDERS & ALERTS**
Set reminder windows for morning, afternoon, and evening readings. Receive gentle notifications to log your blood pressure and take your medications. When a reading is elevated or high, VitaInes alerts you proactively so you can act — not react.

**📅 HISTORY & PDF REPORTS**
Your complete reading history, always at your fingertips. Filter by status, browse by date, and export a clean PDF report to share with your doctor at your next appointment. Your health data, exactly when you need it.

**🌐 ENGLISH & SPANISH**
Full bilingual support — switch between English and Spanish with one tap. Designed for families and communities where language should never be a barrier to good health.

**🔒 SECURE & PRIVATE**
Your health data is encrypted and stored securely in the cloud. Accessible only to you, on your devices. Data is automatically managed to protect your privacy over time.

---

**PERFECT FOR:**
• People managing hypertension or prehypertension
• Patients sharing regular BP logs with their physician
• Anyone wanting to understand their cardiovascular health
• Caregivers monitoring a family member's blood pressure
• Anyone starting a heart-healthy lifestyle

---

**IMPORTANT:**
VitaInes is a personal health tracking and wellness application. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for medical advice.
