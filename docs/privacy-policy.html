VitaInes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how VitaInes collects, uses, and protects your personal and health information when you use our mobile application. By using VitaInes you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Medical Disclaimer: VitaInes is a personal health tracking and wellness application. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for medical advice.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.

Health Data You Enter

Blood pressure readings (systolic, diastolic, pulse), reading timestamps, notes, and classification status that you manually enter into the app.

Medication Information

Names, dosages, schedules, and notes for medications you add to the app.

App Preferences

Language preference, reminder settings, doctor information, and other optional profile fields you choose to provide.

Device Information

Device type and operating system version, used only for app compatibility and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide blood pressure tracking, trend charts, and medication reminders within the app.
  • To generate AI-powered health insights based on your own readings.
  • To send optional reminder notifications you have configured.
  • To allow you to export PDF reports for sharing with your healthcare provider.
  • To restore your data when you sign in on a new device.
  • To improve app reliability and fix bugs.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or health data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), a cloud platform that provides encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

  • Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth — we never store plain-text passwords.
  • Your health data is accessible only to your authenticated account.
  • Firebase services are located in the United States and comply with industry-standard security practices.

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Upon a verified deletion request we will permanently remove your account and health records within 30 days.

5. Third-Party Services

VitaInes uses the following third-party services, each subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and cloud storage
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method (if you choose to use it)
  • Expo / React Native — cross-platform mobile app framework

We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

6. Notifications

With your permission, VitaInes sends local push notifications to remind you to log your blood pressure or take your medications. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app. We do not send promotional or marketing notifications.

7. Children's Privacy

VitaInes is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes we will notify you through the app. Continued use of VitaInes after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
